Hi, I'm Nikhil

Why I do this.

I left home at the age of 10 to go to a world class school. That was my father's dream. He never wanted me to be like the people in our hometown. He wanted me to become something big.

At around 15, I told my father I wanted to be an actor and go to Mumbai. He literally brought the cash home, booked the flights, and said: let's go.

I came back, and started going to Chandigarh to give auditions. I started going on sets. First as a creative intern, then as a creative producer on a reality show. Over the next 2 years, I worked on films like Chamkila (Netflix) and Lafangey (MX Player). By 19 I had a good position in the industry.

The problem was, I didn't want to settle into comfort. I could become an AD like 99% of people, working on other people's visions forever, or be the 1% who have the courage to create something of their own. I chose the 1%.

So I started creating content and building a business where I help other creators. That's what I do.
